# OGSM Profile: Example

## Profile Name

Example OGSM

## Time Horizon

2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30

## Objective

For the leadership team and department heads of this organisation: provide a focused operating rhythm that turns strategy into visible weekly execution, becoming a reliable strategy-to-action management partner — focusing on internal strategy rhythm, not external client advisory or ad-hoc project intake.

## Goals

1. Increase validated MVP workflow completion from 0% baseline to 100% by 2026-06-30. (owner: product owner)
2. Reduce low-alignment meeting hours from 12 hours per week baseline to 9 hours per week by 2026-06-30. (owner: strategy owner)

## Strategies

1. Allocate protected deep work blocks as the primary delivery vehicle for MVP workflow completion, deprioritising ad-hoc collaboration requests.
2. Through calendar load visibility and explicit decision rules, systematically identify and remove low-alignment meeting time.
3. Operate a lightweight weekly MD dashboard with a dedicated owner cadence to surface progress blockers before they compound.

## MD

1. 2026-06-30: MVP workflow completion reaches 100% from 0% baseline, reviewed weekly. validates: S1
2. 2026-06-30: low-alignment meeting hours reduce from 12 hours per week baseline to 9 hours per week, reviewed weekly. validates: S2
3. 2026-06-30: weekly MD review completion rate reaches ≥80% from 0% baseline, reviewed weekly. validates: S3

## MP

1. Product owner, with engineering support, completes dashboard prototype deep work every Monday 10:30-12:00 through 2026-06-30. traces-to: MD1
2. Strategy owner reviews MD baseline every Wednesday 09:00-09:30 through 2026-06-30. traces-to: MD3
3. Calendar owner reviews calendar weekly before Friday OGSM review and cancels or reschedules any exploratory calls that do not map to an active Strategy, through 2026-06-30. traces-to: MD2
4. Strategy owner, with product owner support, builds and publishes lightweight MD dashboard by 2026-05-15. traces-to: MD3

## Review Cadence

Weekly on Friday.

## Decision Rules

- Say no to work that does not support a Strategy or required obligation.
- Add an MD check-in and MP owner before adding new strategy work.
