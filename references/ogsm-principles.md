# OGSM Principles

OGSM connects ambition to execution:

- Objective names the target audience, service scope, value, positioning, and vivid picture of success.
- Goals define concrete outcomes from Objective keywords, with verb, noun, baseline, target or total amount, and date range.
- Strategies define the resources, methodology, and tools selected to reach the Goals. 策略說明為達成目標所選擇的資源配置、方法論與工具，具有取捨性。
- M is the execution check system: MD measures whether the Strategy is working, and MP lists time-ordered action plans.

## Canonical Definitions

- M = 檢核: execution check system.
- MD = 衡量指標: indicators/dashboard checks that validate whether selected resources/Strategies are producing expected results.
- MP = 行動計畫: dated action plans with owner/unit, collaborating unit when relevant, time order, and work content that drive MD movement.

## Canonical Rules

- Goals must include verb, noun, baseline, target or total amount, and date range.
- Goals must follow SMART principles: Specific（具體）、Measurable（可衡量）、Achievable（可實現）、Relevant（呼應 Objective）、Time-bound（有時限）。
- Goals are written in three tiers of completeness:
  - Basic（基礎版）: verb + noun + date + baseline
  - Intermediate（進階版）: Basic + total amount or target
  - Full（最佳版）: Intermediate + allocation or breakdown across owners/units/periods
- MD must include indicator, baseline, target or total amount, date or period, review cadence, and the Strategy it validates.
- Unknown required fields must be marked as missing and clarified with the user, or represented with an explicit proxy, instead of treated as complete.

The core logic check works backward: MP completion should achieve MD; MD achievement should prove Strategy effectiveness; Strategy achievement should reach Goals; Goals should support the Objective.

Strong OGSMs are selective. They make it easier to say no.

## Anti-Patterns

- Objective is a slogan without a tradeoff.
- Goals describe effort instead of outcomes.
- Strategies are task lists instead of resources, methodology, or tools.（策略寫成任務清單，而非資源配置、方法論或工具的選擇）
- Strategies do not identify resources that are new, unique, or consumed.（未說明資源是否具有獨特性或可被消耗）
- Strategies omit methodology or tools that determine how resources are applied.（缺少方法論或工具，無法說明資源如何被運用）
- MD items are vanity metrics or lack indicator, baseline, target or total amount, date or period, review cadence, or Strategy linkage.
- MP items lack owner, collaborating unit when relevant, time order, date or period, or concrete work content.
- MP cannot be traced backward to MD, Strategy, Goal, and Objective.
- Calendar time does not support the declared Strategies.
- Every urgent request is treated as strategy work.
